uTube by kharrison, based on Opaline by verback2308.

- Replies now filters comment notifications from the working YouTube inbox, with support for wrapped comment links and older inbox pages.
- Tap a comment notification to read its parent and replies inside the notification panel. The linked comment is highlighted; you can load more replies and send a reply without opening or changing the playing video.
- Replying to someone inside a thread addresses that person. Drafts are kept separately for each recipient while replies stay in the correct thread.
- Removed the old app-release news setting and stopped release-news polling. Credits remain in Settings; exported debug logs now use the uTube name.
- The casting button stays red whether connected or disconnected.
- Carousel cards fill more of the available height. Swipe left or right between cards, or pull down to refresh; upward vertical scrolling is disabled.

This update keeps uTube's separate app identity and the existing playback and solver configuration. Carousel previews still yield to a playing or minimized video and to AirPlay.
