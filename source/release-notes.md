This is Kurtis's fork of Opaline, based on the original app by verback2308.

- Added **Comment on YouTube** to the video and Shorts comment panels. Write comments and replies through YouTube's page inside Opaline, then tap **Done** to return.
- Refresh the native comment list on return and ignore outdated requests from before the refresh.
- Publish installable updates through Kurtis's AltStore Classic source.

YouTube may ask you to sign in in the browser. This sign-in is separate from the app's account sign-in. Commenting requires an internet connection and a video with comments enabled.
