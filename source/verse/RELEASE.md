Verse 1.0.0 by kharrison

- A dedicated native lyric notebook for iPhone and iPad, installable through AltStore Classic.
- Notebooks, lyric search, tags, favorites, word and line counts, and quick Verse/Chorus/Bridge headings.
- Native spell check with suggestions and a personal dictionary. Optional autocorrect starts off.
- Offline perfect and near rhymes from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, with syllable filters. Select a word in your lyric and tap Rhyme.
- Local autosave and cloud-folder sync through Files. Choose the same iCloud Drive folder in Settings on each device. Sync runs while the app is open; Files handles provider upload/download.
- Immutable saved revisions preserve concurrent edits as separate drafts. Recover lyrics from Recently deleted, share plain text, and export or restore complete backups.

Cloud setup requires choosing a folder in iCloud Drive, not On My iPhone. iCloud propagation and AltStore installation still require confirmation on physical devices. The app does not require a custom CloudKit entitlement or a rhyme API subscription.
